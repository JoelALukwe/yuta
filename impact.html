MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK

Beyond awareness and screening.

A meaningful care cascade follows each clinically indicated next action.

Reach

People attending an event or receiving programme education; not necessarily screened.

Detection

People receiving approved screening or testing.

Clinical action

Treatment initiation, referral or other authorised action where indicated.

Pathway closure

Documented completion of the next clinically indicated action.

EVIDENCE STANDARD

Each number needs context.

For every public metric we record the reporting period, programme, location, definition, source, denominator when relevant, and verification status.

Evidence provenance

A figure should be traceable to an approved source.

Data protection

Patient-identifiable records do not belong in the public website.

Implementation learning

Document what worked, what changed and what remains uncertain.
