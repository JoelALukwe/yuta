IRON IT OUT · THE FIRST STEP

Iron health starts with being heard.

Iron It Out builds awareness among adolescent girls and young women and creates connections from school and community engagement to appropriate care.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Tiredness deserves attention.

Iron deficiency and anaemia can affect wellbeing, learning and everyday life. Awareness is an entry point—not a substitute for professional assessment.

Learn

Age-appropriate conversations about iron health, symptoms and seeking help.

Connect

Work with schools and community partners to establish appropriate referral and screening routes.

Follow through

Connect programme engagement to the wider Maternal Anaemia care pathway.

THE PROGRAMME

From awareness to the next appropriate step.

Programme activities are designed with schools, community organisations and authorised clinical partners.

School workshops

Practical, accessible iron-health education and dialogue.

Community engagement

Trust-building, awareness and links to local services.

Appropriate screening linkage

Screening only where approved and delivered by qualified providers.

Referral and follow-up

Support connection to appropriate clinical next steps.
