SMARTPATH · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Your trust matters. This notice explains what personal information SmartPath collects through its public website, why we use it, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Who we are and the scope of this notice

SmartPath is a South Africa-headquartered social enterprise developing connected, community-led health pathways. This notice applies to the public SmartPath website, including Iron It Out pages, contact forms, partnership enquiries and associated website services. It does not replace the separate notices and consent procedures that healthcare providers, schools, researchers or implementation partners must give for clinical care, screening or research. The precise registered legal entity and Information Officer contact details must be confirmed in the approved version of this notice.

2. Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the name, email address and message that you enter. Your message may also contain your organisation, role or other details you choose to provide. Our hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP addresses, device or browser details, requested pages, timestamps and error or security logs. We do not intentionally collect patient records, clinical histories or diagnostic results through the public contact form.

3. How and why we use information

We use enquiry details to respond to you, discuss partnership or programme opportunities, keep appropriate records of correspondence, maintain and secure the website, troubleshoot technical problems and comply with applicable legal duties. Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), processing must have an applicable lawful justification. The relevant justification depends on the activity, such as responding to your request, a legitimate interest recognised by law, a legal obligation or consent where required. We do not use the public enquiry form as a patient-registration system.

4. Health information, children and safeguarding

Iron It Out includes awareness activities involving school-going adolescent girls and other community members. Health information and children's personal information require heightened safeguards. Do not submit medical records, test results, national identity numbers or information identifying a child through this website. Any school screening, clinical activity, research or referral process must use its own approved consent, safeguarding, clinical-governance and privacy arrangements, including parental or competent-person permissions where required.

5. Who may receive your information

Authorised SmartPath personnel may access relevant correspondence to respond to enquiries. Website hosting, infrastructure, security and email-delivery providers may process information to provide their services. The current website uses Vercel hosting and its contact endpoint is configured to send email through Resend when the required credentials are enabled. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when legally required or where appropriately authorised for a specific purpose.

6. International hosting and transfers

SmartPath operates in an international collaboration environment and website or email service providers may process information outside South Africa. Where POPIA applies, cross-border transfers must satisfy section 72 or another applicable legal requirement. The actual hosting locations, service-provider contracts and transfer safeguards must be verified and documented by SmartPath before final approval of this notice.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information processed, including restricted administrative access and managed hosting. No website or transmission is completely secure. We assess suspected security incidents and will provide legally required notifications when applicable. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or clinical information through public forms.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain enquiry correspondence and relevant technical records only for as long as reasonably necessary for their stated purposes, legitimate operational requirements or applicable legal obligations. A specific organisational retention schedule has not yet been verified; it must be approved and implemented rather than assumed. When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted or de-identified as appropriate.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we hold personal information about you, request access, correction or deletion, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the basis of processing, and raise a complaint with the South African Information Regulator. Requests may require reasonable identity verification. You can start a request through the Partner With Us contact route, marking the message “Privacy request” and avoiding unnecessary sensitive details.

10. Cookies and external services

The website may rely on essential browser storage or technical cookies needed for operation, including user interface preferences. External maps, videos or embedded resources may have their own privacy practices and may receive technical connection information. We have not verified a complete cookie and tracking inventory, so this notice does not claim that analytics, advertising cookies or third-party tracking are absent. A cookie audit and any legally required preference controls should be completed before final approval.

11. Updates and privacy contact

We may revise this notice when website functionality, providers or legal obligations change. The date at the top indicates the current published revision. For a privacy question or rights request, use the SmartPath Partner With Us form. The designated Information Officer's direct contact and the registered responsible-party details will be added following formal verification.

Questions or requests?

Contact SmartPath using our enquiry form. Please do not include patient records or sensitive health information.
