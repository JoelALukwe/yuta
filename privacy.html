SMARTPATH · WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy

How SmartPath handles information submitted through this website.

Updated 19 November 2026 · Subject to final organisational and legal review

Who this notice covers

This notice concerns the public SmartPath website, including programme pages and the Partner With Us enquiry form. It does not govern patient records or clinical services operated by independent healthcare providers.

Information you choose to send

When you contact us, you may provide your name, organisation, email address, area of interest and message. Please do not send patient-identifiable information, medical records or sensitive health details through this website.

Why we use enquiry information

We use submitted contact details to read and respond to enquiries, explore potential collaborations and manage related correspondence. We do not describe this website as a clinical intake or patient registration service.

Website technology and service providers

The website is hosted using third-party infrastructure. Contact form delivery, when enabled, uses an email delivery service. Technical records needed to operate and protect the site may be processed by hosting and delivery providers. The exact provider arrangements and retention periods should be confirmed before this notice is treated as final.

Sharing and retention

Enquiry information may be accessible to authorised SmartPath personnel and service providers involved in handling the enquiry. A documented retention schedule and applicable legal bases require organisational approval; no specific retention period is asserted here.

Your choices and requests

You can ask about personal information you have sent, request a correction or raise a privacy concern using the Partner With Us contact route. Applicable rights depend on the relevant law and circumstances.

Children and health information

Iron It Out includes education for school-going teenage girls. This public site is not designed to collect children's medical information. School activities and any clinical data collection require separate consent, safeguarding and information-governance arrangements.

Policy status

This is an operational website privacy disclosure prepared from the currently verified site functionality. SmartPath's legal adviser must review and approve the final jurisdiction-specific notice, including the responsible entity, contact details, processing grounds, international transfers and retention periods.

Get in touch

For questions or feedback, use our contact form. Please do not submit patient or sensitive health information.
