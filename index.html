THE CHALLENGE

Care should not end at a handoff.

People can be screened without receiving treatment, referred without reaching care, or lost before follow-up. SmartPath focuses on the connections that make the journey complete.

Disconnected entry points

People enter through schools, communities and facilities, but services may not be linked.

Missing handoffs

Testing, clinical decisions and referrals need clear ownership and coordination.

Invisible follow-up

Without visibility into the next action, care gaps can remain unresolved.

OUR APPROACH

Community. Science. Technology.

Three connected disciplines, one accountable pathway.

Community

Co-design with people and frontline teams; build trust, awareness, navigation and early entry.

Science

Use clinical evidence and implementation learning to define what works in real settings.

Technology

Use appropriate data tools to make referrals, follow-up and pathway completion visible.

HOW IT WORKS

From fragmentation to a functioning pathway.

A repeatable five-stage method that strengthens existing health systems.

01 · Map the pathway

Identify late entry, drop-offs, delays and broken connections.

02 · Co-design the package

Agree roles, clinical rules, safeguards, diagnostics and workflows.

03 · Create early entry

Build awareness and appropriate access to detection.

04 · Connect action

Link results to treatment, referral and documented follow-up.

05 · Improve and embed

Use evidence and cost learning to support routine adoption.
