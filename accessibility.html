Accessibility

Our approach to making SmartPath's website usable by as many people as possible.

Updated 19 November 2026 · Subject to final organisational and legal review

Our commitment

SmartPath aims to make its digital information usable across devices and by people using assistive technology. We seek clear language, meaningful structure and access to important information without relying on animation or visual effects.

Features currently designed into the site

The website includes a skip-to-content link, labelled navigation and controls, responsive layouts, alternative text for many images, a theme switch and reduced-motion styling. These features are design intentions, not a claim of full conformance.

Known areas for continued testing

Interactive maps, media, hover effects, colour contrast, form errors and keyboard focus require ongoing testing. The site has not been independently certified as conforming to WCAG 2.2 AA.

Alternative ways to access information

Where a map or interactive feature cannot be used, the location information should remain available as text. If content is difficult to access, contact us through the Partner With Us page and describe the page and barrier encountered.

Feedback and improvements

Accessibility feedback helps prioritise fixes. Please include the page address, what you were trying to do and any assistive technology details you wish to share; avoid including sensitive health information.

Review status

This statement describes current design goals and limitations. Formal accessibility testing, remediation and a review date are still required before claiming a particular compliance level.

Get in touch

For questions or feedback, use our contact form. Please do not submit patient or sensitive health information.
