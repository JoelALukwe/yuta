Accessibility Statement

SmartPath is working to make information about connected care accessible, understandable and usable across devices and abilities.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Our commitment

We aim to provide an inclusive digital experience for visitors, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, magnification, alternative input methods or reduced-motion settings. Accessibility matters especially for public-health information, community engagement and programmes involving young people.

2. Standard we are working toward

Our accessibility improvement target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). We have not completed an independent conformance audit and do not currently claim full WCAG 2.2 AA compliance or certification.

3. Accessibility features

The website is designed with semantic page structure, labelled navigation, responsive layouts, descriptive image text where provided, keyboard-operable standard links and controls, and reduced-motion support for certain animated effects. Light and dark visual themes are available. These features require ongoing verification on real devices and assistive technologies.

4. Known limitations and areas under review

Interactive geographic maps, some image galleries, moving visual elements, embedded media, keyboard focus behaviour, colour contrast and dynamically updated content may present barriers. Captions and transcripts for all media have not been fully verified. The content administration interface also requires further accessibility testing. We are not presenting untested features as fully accessible.

5. Accessible alternatives

Where an interactive map is difficult to use, the Where We Work page also presents location information in text-based cards. If you cannot access a photograph, video, document, form or interactive element, contact SmartPath and request the information in a suitable alternative format. We will review requests and respond as our available resources permit.

6. How to report an accessibility barrier

Use the Partner With Us form and begin your message with “Accessibility feedback”. Please tell us which page or feature was affected, what you were trying to do and the type of assistance or alternative you need. You may optionally describe your browser or assistive technology. Do not include medical records or sensitive personal information.

7. Ongoing testing and improvement

We intend to review key user journeys, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, headings, image descriptions, forms, mobile layouts, colour contrast, media alternatives and reduced-motion behaviour. Findings should be prioritised for remediation, retested and documented. No formal testing completion date or conformance result is asserted here.

8. Statement updates

This statement will be updated as accessibility testing and remediation progress. It reflects our current commitments and known limitations rather than a guarantee that every feature is barrier-free.

Questions or requests?

Contact SmartPath using our enquiry form. Please do not include patient records or sensitive health information.
