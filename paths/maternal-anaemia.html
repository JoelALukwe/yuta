THE CONNECTED CARE LOOP

Four steps. One accountable pathway.

Each step is designed with the community and clinical teams responsible for safe delivery.

01 · EARLY ENTRY

Iron It Out

School and community iron-health awareness, education and appropriate referral routes.

Explore programme
02 · DETECT EARLY

Hb and ferritin

Point-of-care haemoglobin and ferritin assessment where approved and clinically appropriate, delivered by authorised teams.

03 · TREAT AND REASSESS

Clinical action

Connect findings to authorised oral iron or other treatment decisions and a planned reassessment, typically around four weeks when clinically appropriate.

04 · ESCALATE AND CLOSE

Referral completion

Support referral to higher-level care, including consideration of IV iron by qualified clinicians when indicated, and document follow-through.

PATHWAY DESIGN

Strengthening existing services.
